Satellite lines of helium-like ions in strongly coupled plasma environment

Journal: Journal of Quantitative Spectroscopy and Radiative Transfer (Elsevier)

Date: June 29, 2021

We report the transition energies and probabilities of satellite lines due to transitions of He-like ions embedded in a strongly coupled plasma (SCP) environment. Using the ion-sphere (IS) model to simulate SCP, both relativistic (MCDF using GRASP2K) and non-relativistic (Hylleraas basis) results are presented. Good agreement with experimental data is observed. An empirical relation to estimate partial pressure in dense plasma is also proposed.

Ps− and H₂⁺ Ions under Spatial Confinement

Journal: RGN Publication

Date: March 27, 2018

Ground state energy eigenvalues of spatially confined Ps⁻ and H₂⁺ ions are computed using the Rayleigh-Ritz variational method in a spherical box. Hylleraas basis functions capture electron and proton correlations. Critical confinement radii and destabilization pressures are reported.

Explicitly correlated variational estimates of the energy levels of negative hydrogen ion under spatial confinement

Journal: International Journal of Quantum Chemistry (Wiley)

Date: February 7, 2018

Using the Ritz method and Hylleraas-type basis, this study analyzes structural modifications of the H⁻ ion in a confined spherical region. Ground and excited states, including quasi-bound singly excited states, are reported, alongside thermodynamic pressure effects and angular correlation analysis.

Structural properties of lithium atom under weakly coupled plasma environment

Journal: Physics of Plasmas (AIP)

Date: April 19, 2016

This work investigates lithium atoms in a weakly coupled plasma simulated via Debye-Hückel potential. Structural changes, ionization potential reduction, and variations in physical properties like dielectric susceptibility and magnetic shielding with plasma density are discussed using Hylleraas expansions.
